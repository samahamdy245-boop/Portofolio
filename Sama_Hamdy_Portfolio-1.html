About

01

Sama is an Electrical & Telecommunications Engineering student at the Higher Institute of Engineering, Zagazig, currently building a UI/UX design practice alongside her engineering coursework. Her design training runs from Google's Foundations of UX Design course through a dedicated UI/UX program at the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI), and she has put that training to work designing a complete 30-screen mobile app and a separate website UX case study.

Outside of design, she has been steadily building a broader technical and professional base — networking fundamentals, embedded C programming, Microsoft Office proficiency, and a business-thinking program with McKinsey.org — while staying active in Egypt's tech community through events like Build with AI: Masr Edition and the Creativa Zagazig Business Event.

Her design work lives on Behance, and her networking capstone project is documented on GitHub.
